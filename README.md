## 📌 Project Overview
**BusSync** is an intelligent multimedia transit content delivery device designed to enhance passenger experience and address acoustic discomfort in Sri Lankan intercity public buses. 

By integrating ambient noise monitoring, adaptive acoustic masking, offline edge content delivery, and synchronized multi-display transit infotainment, BusSync provides a distraction-free, reliable, and localized passenger transit journey.

---

## 🎯 Key Research Objectives
1. **Adaptive Acoustic Masking:** Dynamically monitoring ambient decibel levels inside public buses to deliver adaptive, fatigue-reducing acoustic masking.
2. **Edge Content Delivery & Caching:** Offline micro-caching architecture enabling continuous infotainment playback across cellular dead zones.
3. **Display Synchronization:** Low-latency synchronization across multiple in-vehicle screens for route milestones and passenger notices.
4. **Context-Aware Information Delivery:** Geolocation-aware route alerts, safety reminders, and localized passenger notifications.
